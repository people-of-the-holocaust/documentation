\section{Data Requirements}

\begin{longtable}{|p{0.25\textwidth}|p{0.65\textwidth}|}
	\hline
	\textbf{Data Item}     & \textbf{Description}                                                                                                                \\
	\hline
	People Records         & records containing person identifiers, first names, last names, alternative names, titles, places, organizations, and designations. \\
	\hline
	Place Records          & Records containing place identifiers, names, types, subtypes, countries, and location identifiers.                                  \\
	\hline
	Activity Records       & Records connecting people, places, actions, and supporting sentence/paragraph details.                                              \\
	\hline
	Map Records            & Records containing place names, latitude, longitude, type, subtype, and current country for map display.                            \\
	\hline
	Article Source Records & Records identifying encyclopedia articles processed by the interpreter.                                                             \\
	\hline
	Error Logs             & Records of source articles or processing steps that failed during interpreter execution.                                            \\
	\hline
\end{longtable}

The database will be the primary source for website data. The CSV and JSON files will be used during development, testing, or migration, but they are not the intended long-term data source.
